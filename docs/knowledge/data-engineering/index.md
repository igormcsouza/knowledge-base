# Data Engineering

Notes on data warehouses, ETL/ELT pipelines, and the platform concerns that
surround moving and transforming data at scale — a category for engineering
knowledge that sits closer to the data platform than to any single
application's database.

## Articles

- [BigQuery and Modern ETL/ELT](bigquery-modern-etl-elt.md) — BigQuery as an
  analytical warehouse, ETL vs. ELT, raw/staging/curated layering, batch vs.
  streaming vs. CDC ingestion, Dataform, incremental processing, idempotency,
  orchestration, warehouse modeling, partitioning/clustering, and
  observability.
